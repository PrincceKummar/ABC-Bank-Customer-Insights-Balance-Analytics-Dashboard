# ABC Bank Customer Insights Balance Analytics Dashboard

🚀 **Turning Raw Data into Actionable Insights with Power BI** 📊

I'm excited to share a recent project where I built a **dynamic banking dashboard** using **Power BI**, transforming raw customer data into a clear, visually compelling, and insight-driven report for ABC Bank.

🔍 **What this dashboard delivers:**

✅ A snapshot of **$236.93M in total customer balances**  
✅ Segmentation of **3,994 customers** by **gender**, **job classification**, and **state**  
✅ Interactive **geographical breakdown** of customers across the U.S.  
✅ Quick visibility into the **Top 5 and Bottom 5 customers by balance** – supporting client relationship strategies  
✅ Intuitive donut charts and bar visuals for fast, data-driven decision making

📌 **Key Insights:**
- Nearly **49% of customers are white-collar professionals**, hinting at strong engagement from this segment  
- Customer base is **slightly male-dominant** (54%)  
- The **top 5 customers alone hold over $39M**, indicating potential for targeted high-value services

💡 **Tools Used:** Power BI | DAX | Data Modeling | Visual Storytelling

This dashboard isn’t just about aesthetics—it’s about enabling **strategic decisions** through real-time analytics and clear visuals. Whether you're in banking, retail, or any data-rich industry, tools like Power BI can transform how you understand and act on your data.
